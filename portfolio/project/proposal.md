{(questions|}

- Which matters more in decision making, confidence in one's own answer or the majority opinion?
- How does the type of cognitive task affect conformity behaviour?
- Does conformity lead to errors more often than to correct answers?

{|questions)}

{(datasources|}

I will get my data from a dataset "Sex differences in confidence influence patterns of conformity" by University of St Andrews School of Psychology and Neuroscience. It contains two Excel files with experimental data from partisipants who completed two different cognitive tasks. Dataset includes information such as answers, confidence levels and other people responses.

I cleaned and filtered the original Excel files, selected relevant variables for my research questions, and combined them in one CSV dataset. My final dataset contains 3,228 observations from 72 participants who completed both tasks.

Initial dataset:
https://research-portal.st-andrews.ac.uk/en/datasets/dataset-sex-differences-in-confidence-influence-patterns-of-confo/

Cleaned, filtered and combined dataset:
[Conformity_Data.csv](https://github.com/EkaterinaArutyunyants/data)

{|datasources)}

{(justification|}

Confotmity became an important phenomenon in society, especially among young people. People change their mind, potentially lose their individuality under the influence of the majoty opinion.

My aim is to research whether confidence in one's own answer can help to resist the majority, whether conformity behaviour depends on the type of cognitive tasks, and mainly does conformity leads to positive or negative outcomes. I chose this dataset because it allows me to do research in conformity based on participants confidence in answer, opinions of others and desicion accurency.

The initial dataset contains raw experimental data which is difficult to analyse, identify patterns, and give a conclusions. For my research, I extracted and orginised the relevant information and created single dataset. However, to analyse and find patterns for this information, data visualisation is crucial. It will allow to understand what happened during experiments, explore how different factors affect conformity behaviour, and find out its consequences.

{|justification)}
